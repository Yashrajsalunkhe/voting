# Sample Excel File Structure

Place your Excel files in the respective year folders with this structure:

## Example: second-year-students.xlsx

| A (URN) | B (Name) | C (Mother Name) |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| 2022AI001 | Rahul Kumar | Sunita Kumar |
| 2022AI002 | Priya Sharma | Meera Sharma |
| 2022AI003 | Amit Patel | Kavya Patel |
